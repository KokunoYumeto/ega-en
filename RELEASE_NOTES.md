# English edition of 8 September 2026

This edition updates the complete linked reader and the standalone readers for EGA III-2 and EGA IV-4 from the validated current English text. It covers EGA I with Chapter 0, EGA II, EGA III-1 and III-2, and EGA IV-1 through IV-4. The standalone readers for EGA IV-1, IV-2 and IV-3 are carried forward unchanged.

The English edition incorporates documented corrections while retaining the distinction between the printed French witness and the current translated text. The separate French edition remains diplomatic.

The editable TeX archive contains the inputs used by all the included readers, including the bibliography database omitted from the previous source archive. Provenance records explain the source witnesses, editorial decisions and checks behind this edition.
