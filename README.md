The cumulative reader is accompanied by standalone volumes, complete editable LaTeX, and reproducible source projects. Separately labelled printed-witness translations preserve the historical reading.

## Editorial scope

This edition incorporates the accumulated accepted corrections to notation, hypotheses, proof passages and references in the English sources, including EGA 0, III and IV. They appear in the cumulative reader and all affected standalone volumes. The two unaffected standalone editions—III, Part One printed-witness translation, and III, Part Two—retain their previous PDF and source bytes. The original-language French edition remains a separate publication; this release does not certify a new French reader or claim that the multilingual correction backlog is complete.

The source changes were checked against the admitted corrections with exact inverse replay. Rebuilt readers passed deterministic replay, actual dependency checks, bounded formula/content/reference review and complete-page visual inspection. Two missing-line rendering defects and two contents destinations were repaired. The modular project reproduces the released cumulative PDF; its complete assembled LaTeX retains one disclosed inherited page-699 typography/link-box difference, without a mathematical or text difference. The standalone native and assembled forms agree byte-for-byte. Later reports are preserved separately and are not silently included.
