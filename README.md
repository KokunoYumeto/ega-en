# Elements of Algebraic Geometry — Cumulative English Edition

The cumulative reader is accompanied by seven standalone editions, complete directly downloadable LaTeX, and reproducible source projects. The separately labelled III-1 printed-witness translation preserves the historical reading.

## Editorial scope and verification

This edition incorporates the admitted corrections to notation, hypotheses, proof passages and references in the English sources. The cumulative reader and affected standalone volumes IV-2, IV-3 and IV-4 have been rebuilt and checked. III-1 current, III-1 printed-witness, III-2 and IV-1 retain their exact previously published reader/source bytes because their dependencies are unchanged.

The supplied modular project reproduces the released cumulative PDF. Its direct assembled LaTeX is complete and independently validated; it retains one inherited typography and link-box difference at displayed page 700, with no text or mathematical difference. All visible numeric contents links were checked, and the page-827 missing-line rendering defect is repaired. The three rebuilt standalone native and assembled forms agree byte-for-byte.

IV-4 standalone retains its inherited repeated backmatter contents entries and has no PDF hyperlinks. Use the complete cumulative reader for linked navigation. These limitations are disclosed, not represented as a new defect-free contents edition.

Each PDF is followed by its complete LaTeX and reproducible source ZIP. The archives include the native modular inputs, styles, macros, bibliographies, build scripts and dependency information. Standard TeX distribution packages and fonts are documented external dependencies; no unpublished project text is needed.

Reader/source assembly, correction integration, bounded validation, and rendering and navigation repairs for this update: OpenAI Codex — GPT-6 Astra, Ultra effort. Retained III-1 adoption, diplomatic reconstruction, assembly, correction and audit: GPT-5.6 Sol, Ultra effort. These credits describe the recorded work, not the entire inherited translation. Earlier provenance remains in the source evidence and edition history; not every historical model or effort level is established. No independent human editing, review, approval or certification is implied.

The French diplomatic and corrected/current editions remain separate. Earlier English editions and every inherited public file remain accessible. This release does not claim that all multilingual corrections are finished, and grants no new rights over the original text.
