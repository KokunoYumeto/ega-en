# Translation sources and contributors

The original *Éléments de géométrie algébrique* is by Alexandre Grothendieck and Jean Dieudonné.

Substantial parts of the English text and TeX framework in this edition derive from the human community translation [ryankeleti/ega](https://github.com/ryankeleti/ega), including the source version [3d7afe28c99d1887ccdb85acf2ca1afd93fb1971](https://github.com/ryankeleti/ega/tree/3d7afe28c99d1887ccdb85acf2ca1afd93fb1971).

That project's [contributor list](https://github.com/ryankeleti/ega/blob/3d7afe28c99d1887ccdb85acf2ca1afd93fb1971/CONTRIBUTORS) names **Mateo Carmona, Tim Holzschuh, Tim Hosgood, Ryan Keleti, Thiago Solovera & Nery, and Wayne Yang**. Its [section credits](https://github.com/ryankeleti/ega/blob/3d7afe28c99d1887ccdb85acf2ca1afd93fb1971/README.md) and [commit history](https://github.com/ryankeleti/ega/commits/3d7afe28c99d1887ccdb85acf2ca1afd93fb1971/) record the individual contributions, including contributions beyond that list.

The inherited English wording includes the following parts, with subsequent editorial changes:

| Part | English text inherited from the community translation |
| --- | --- |
| EGA 0 | Sections 1–10, portions of section 11, and section 14 |
| EGA I | Sections 1–10 |
| EGA II | Sections 1–8 |
| EGA III | Sections 1 and 3 |
| EGA IV | Section 16, the opening and section 17.1, and the opening and section 20.1 |

The upstream project is an incomplete translation of the full EGA corpus. The cumulative edition also contains later translation work, additions, mathematical corrections, reference repairs and typesetting. The upstream contributors are credited for their own work; they are not represented as authors or reviewers of those later additions. Reused translation remains their contribution even where it has been edited.

Subsequent source preparation, correction and assembly include AI-assisted work by OpenAI Codex, GPT-6 Astra, Ultra; the EGA III-1 sources also include work by GPT-5.6 Sol, Ultra. These credits concern those contributions, not authorship of the inherited human translation. No endorsement by the upstream contributors, the original authors, IHÉS or NUMDAM is implied.

The cited upstream snapshot has no repository-wide licence notice covering its translation text. Its `scripts/README.md` identifies separate Stacks Project scripts and links their licence; that notice does not designate the EGA translation as open-licensed.
