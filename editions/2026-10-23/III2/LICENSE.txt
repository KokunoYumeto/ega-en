ORIGINAL WORK AND COMMUNITY TRANSLATION
========================================

The original work is by Alexandre Grothendieck and Jean Dieudonné.
Retained English text and TeX derive in part from the human community
translation https://github.com/ryankeleti/ega . Its named contributors include
Mateo Carmona, Tim Holzschuh, Tim Hosgood, Ryan Keleti, Thiago Solovera & Nery,
and Wayne Yang. See ATTRIBUTION.md for the exact source version and scope.

The additional translation is AI-translated. Subsequent corrections, editing, typesetting and assembly are AI-produced. Recorded work includes OpenAI Codex, GPT-6 Astra, Ultra; the EGA III-1 sources also include GPT-5.6 Sol, Ultra. The publisher did not translate the text. The inherited human translation remains the work of its credited contributors; it is not attributed to the AI models.
