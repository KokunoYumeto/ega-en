- Publishes the current 1411-page complete linked reader and five current
  standalone readers for EGA III-2 and IV-1 through IV-4.
- Binds all readers to the exact 134-file active English build-input
  set at Canon R65.
- Adds the serialized build, PDF-structure, and visual-QA evidence for the R65
  reconstruction.

This release remains an independent English edition and contains no bilingual,
parallel, paired, side-by-side, or interleaved reader.
